# Voice and tone

Applies to all user-facing copy and to every LLM prompt that writes text for the user.

- **Calm and sincere, with a little geeky humor.** Warm, direct, never preachy.
- Git and testing language is a light theme ("commit", "conflict", "tests passing"), used where it
  helps and never required to understand the app.
- Honest, not harsh. Describe the gap between words and actions without shaming. Prefer curiosity
  ("What got in the way?") to judgment.
- Future-self letters are written in first person, from the future self to the user. They use the
  user's own phrases and writing style, and refer to real results from the report card. They must
  not invent facts, events, or numbers that are not in the provided data.
- The 1, 5, and 20 year selves each care about a different value the user declared, so they can
  disagree. They stay kind while disagreeing.
- No medical, financial, or mental health advice. If a user's entry suggests crisis, respond with
  care and point to local support resources instead of continuing the game.
- Keep copy short. One idea per sentence.

# Voice and tone

Applies to all user-facing copy and to every LLM prompt that writes text for the user.

- **Warm, encouraging, and a little playful.** Like a kind friend who believes in you. Never preachy.
- **Plain everyday words.** The audience is not technical. Never use software terms in the UI:
  no commit, git, merge, branch, test, build, claim, or conflict. Use these instead:

  | Concept in code                     | Word in the UI                      |
  | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
  | Goal                                | Dream                               |
  | Claim                               | Little step                         |
  | Entry                               | Page (journal page)                 |
  | Conflict                            | Nudge                               |
  | Council / future selves             | Future Me, letters                  |
  | Value (ambition, connection, peace) | Life area (Growth, Love, Wellbeing) |

- **Celebrate first.** Mention a win before anything that is behind. Status labels are
  "Done!", "Almost there", "Keep going", and "Not started".
- Honest, not harsh. When words and actions don't line up, say so gently and ask a curious question
  ("What tiny version could you try tomorrow?"). Never shame.
- Future Me letters are first person, from the future self to the user. They use the user's own
  phrases and refer only to real data. They must not invent facts, events, or numbers.
- The three future selves each look after a different life area, so they can disagree. They stay
  kind while disagreeing.
- No medical, financial, or mental health advice. If an entry suggests crisis, respond with care and
  point to local support instead of continuing.
- Keep copy short. One idea per sentence.
